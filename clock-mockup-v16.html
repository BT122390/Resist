Slant Board Squats

Wednesday · Block A · paired with Concept2 RowErg Intervals
Prescription
4 rounds @ weighted vest 10–15 lb, one set per round alternating with RowErg
Last result · Sep 16
4×12 @ 15 lb vest, full depth
Coaching notes
Heels elevated on the slant board, ankle locked in dorsiflexion — the plan's most direct simulation of steep-descent quad loading. Full depth, 3s descent, brief pause without relaxing, then drive up.
P2 W1–6: 4×12 @ vest 10–15 lb. P2 W7–12: 4×15 @ vest 15–20 lb. P3: 4×12 @ vest 20–25 lb, or add a 2–3s pause once vest weight plateaus. Never increase load and add a pause in the same week. If knee pain develops, reduce the slant angle before reducing load.
