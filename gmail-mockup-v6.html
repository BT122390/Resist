Prescription

4 rounds @ weighted vest 10–15 lb — one set per round, alternating with Concept2 RowErg Intervals (Block A).

Coaching notes

Feet on an inclined slant board, heels elevated, toes down-slope — this locks the ankle into dorsiflexion and shifts load onto the quad tendon at close to the ankle angle used descending steep terrain. The plan's most direct simulation of that loading pattern.

Full depth, controlled 3s descent, brief pause at the bottom without relaxing, then drive up.

P2 W1–6: 4×12 @ vest 10–15 lb. P2 W7–12: 4×15 @ vest 15–20 lb. P3: 4×12 @ vest 20–25 lb, or add a 2–3s pause instead of load once vest weight plateaus.

Never increase load and add a pause in the same week. If knee pain develops, reduce the slant angle before reducing load.
